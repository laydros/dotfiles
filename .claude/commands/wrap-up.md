End-of-session wrap-up: update memory before Jason closes out.

Be selective. The bar for saving is high — most sessions produce nothing worth keeping. A short "nothing new this session" is a perfectly good outcome.

## Memory

The four types, the de-duplication rule, and the list of what not to save are built-in defaults. Follow them as written; they aren't repeated here.

Two things they don't cover:

- If you're unsure whether something is worth keeping, it isn't.
- Write the saves yourself. Don't ask permission for bookkeeping — report what you saved after the fact, one line each.


## Output

Keep it terse. List what you saved to memory, or "nothing worth keeping". Don't write a session summary.
